Living Water Express

Six sites, Denver metro area · Acquired October 2021

Cobblestone acquired the six Living Water Express sites across the Denver metro (725 E 144th Ave, Thornton; 130 S Wadsworth Blvd, Lakewood; 857 S Havana St, Aurora; 6646 S Wadsworth Blvd, Littleton; 6875 S Broadway, Littleton; 5651 County Line Pl, Highlands Ranch), in October 2021. Based on public, mobile-location visit data, estimated monthly volume across the group increased 51% year-over-year on average, comparing the 12 months before acquisition to the first 12 months under Cobblestone ownership.
