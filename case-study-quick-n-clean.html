Quick N Clean

Three sites, Northglenn, CO and Scottsdale, AZ · Acquired Mar, Jul 2021

Cobblestone acquired three Quick N Clean sites (11495 Washington St, Northglenn, CO, 725 E 144th Ave, Thornton, CO 80023 and 7501 E Indian School Rd, Scottsdale, AZ), in March and July 2021. Based on public, mobile-location visit data (Advan), estimated monthly volume across the group increased 79% year-over-year on average, comparing the 12 months before acquisition to the first 12 months under Cobblestone ownership.
